Research Framework

Understanding the Shopping Environment

Store atmosphere refers to the physical environment and visual attributes of an establishment. It shapes how customers perceive products, navigate spaces, and feel during their shopping experience.

1

Store Display

Refers to how merchandise is organized, categorized, and visually presented on shelves or counter racks.

  • Category groupings (e.g. cosmetics vs pharmacy)
  • Eye-level promotional shelf placements
  • Clear price tagging and labels
2

Store Design & Layout

Encompasses spatial layout, aisle widths, counter locations, and overall foot-traffic flow inside the store.

  • Unobstructed walkway width
  • Convenient checkout placement
  • Accessible rack heights
3

Overall Appearance

Ambient elements such as lighting intensity, temperature control, floor cleanliness, and background environment.

  • Well-lit, bright interior lighting
  • Clean floors and dust-free counters
  • Comfortable ambient air temperature

How Store Atmosphere Influences Purchasing Behavior

A comfortable and organized store environment encourages customers to spend more time exploring available products. Well-arranged merchandise catches shoppers' attention, while proper lighting, air conditioning, and cleanliness enhance customer satisfaction and store loyalty within Barangay San Bartolome.
